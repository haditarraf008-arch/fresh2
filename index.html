Sizing & Condition Guide

How We Measure Vintage Garments

Tag sizes on vintage clothing often vary from modern sizing. We provide flat measurements for accurate fitting:

Spec Description
Pit to Pit (P2P) Measured straight across chest from one armpit seam to the other.
Length Measured from top collar seam down to bottom hemline.
Waist & Inseam Pants measured flat across waist line and along inner leg seam.

Condition Scale

10/10 Mint: Like-new or unused vintage condition.

9/10 Excellent: Clean vintage condition with minor natural age.

8/10 Good: Authentic vintage fading or distressing adding character.
